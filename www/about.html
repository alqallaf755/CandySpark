Sparking Curiosity AboutElectricity⚡

the goal is to make learning
about electricity wonderful!

At CandySpark I think learning about electricity should be fun like going on an adventure. It should not be boring. I want to make kids curious about electricity and help them like science. I do this with lessons that kids can do themselves and experiments that are hands-on. I made CandySpark by myself I am Shahad Alqallaf. I did it for my Year 3 Electrical & Electronic Engineering project. CandySpark makes electricity easy to understand it breaks it down into steps that kids can follow and learn from without feeling scared. Sometimes electricity can be confusing it can even be a little scary. That is why I tried to make CandySpark a happy and safe place for kids to learn. It is colourful and fun. It helps kids want to learn more. Come with me on this journey and see how cool science can be. I want to show you that science can be fun, safe and easy for everyone to learn. CandySpark is about electricity and it is made for kids, like you.

⚡ Always remember: Safety first!

Ask an adult before trying any electrical experiments.
