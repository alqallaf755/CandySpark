Sparking Curiosity About Electricity ⚡

Our mission is to make learning
about electricity fun and easy!

At CandySpark, we believe that learning about electricity should be an exciting adventure! Our interactive lessons and experiments aim to ignite curiosity and foster a love of science among children.

Designed and developed by Shahad Alqallaf as part of her Year 3 Electrical & Electronic Engineering project, CandySpark breaks down electrical concepts into simple, fun, and engaging steps that help young learners build confidence.

We also recognise that electricity can seem confusing or even intimidating which is why this platform focuses on safe, friendly and colourful learning that encourages discovery rather than fear.

Join us on this electrifying journey and explore the exciting side of science in a way that’s engaging, safe and accessible for everyone!

⚡ Always remember: Safety first!

Ask an adult before trying any electrical experiments.
